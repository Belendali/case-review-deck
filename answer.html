Chapter 01 · Growth

Answer.AI

From a shared answer machine to a personalized school — how we found growth after hitting the wall.

Bella (Yuanyuan) Li · Product Designer · Aug 2024 – present

The product

Scan any problem,
learn from the answer.

Answer.AI is an AI learning app: students photograph a homework problem and get an instant, step-by-step solution — then tutors, flashcards and study tools around it.

7 million students. I led the app's full redesign, from direction to ship.

→
Act I · The honeymoon

Scan & Solve nailed a moment of need.

Instant answers at homework time — the loop was simple and the top of the funnel loved it. Signups climbed toward a million registered students.

The old app · five screens
Act II · The wall

By 2024, the answer machine was winning — and stalling.

Force 01

Leaky retention

Students solved and left. Nothing in the loop asked them to come back tomorrow.

Force 02

A commoditized loop

Every competitor had the same scan-to-answer machine. Same feature, same ads, same users.

Force 03

A new AI window

LLMs made answers a commodity overnight — and opened a much bigger door than answers.

The diagnosis

People came for answers.
Nothing asked them to stay.

We were a transactional tool in a relationship business. Staying an answer machine meant staying replaceable.

Decision · What's on the table

Three ways out. We could only afford one.

Option A

Double down on solver accuracy

Safe, measurable — but polishing a commodity. Everyone's answers were getting good.

Option B

Gamify retention

Streaks and points buy visits, not value. Borrowed time on a transactional loop.

Option C · The bet

Rebuild around the learner

Slower and riskier — but the only path that compounds and can't be copied in a sprint.

Make the answer worth learning from.

Two bets: explanations you can learn from, in a chat calm enough to think in — step-by-step logic instead of a text wall, tools out of the way until needed.

Reflection

What I'd do differently.

Our interviews over-sampled the students who loved us — the signal on "quiet quitters" came late. Next time I'd instrument silence as carefully as praise.
