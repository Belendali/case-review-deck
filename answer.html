Chapter 01 · Growth

Answer.AI

From a shared answer machine to a personalized school — how we found growth after hitting the wall.

Bella (Yuanyuan) Li · Product Designer · Aug 2024 – present

The product

Scan any problem,
learn from the answer.

Answer.AI is an AI learning app: students photograph a homework problem and get an instant, step-by-step solution — then tutors, flashcards and study tools around it.

7 million students. I led the app's full redesign, from direction to ship.

The honeymoon · 2023 — 2024

We landed in a golden window.

Free chatbots couldn't take a photo yet — GPT-4o only opened image and file upload to free users in May 2024, with caps. Snap a problem, get the steps: we owned that moment, and so did Question AI and Gauth — all of us in the App Store top 15.

Curve illustrative · milestones are real
The turn · late 2024

Growth began to slow.

The capability that opened the market became table stakes — and our one-and-done loop gave students little reason to return. Retention slipped with it.

Outside

Multimodal AI went mainstream

Free ChatGPT could read photos by May 2024.

Outside

Free alternatives multiplied

Inside

The loop ended at the answer

Acquisition, but no learning habit.

The diagnosis

Fewer students chose us.
Most left after one answer.

Scan & Solve was no longer unique. Students got what they needed quickly, but rarely stayed long enough to build a habit with Answer.AI.

The decision

Daily learning became our first priority.

It was the most frequent need, closest to existing user behavior, and the strongest foundation for future growth.

The goal was to move from solving one question to supporting how students learn every day.

Layer 02 · Stay

Give students a reason to come back tomorrow.

We kept only the methods with proven demand: daily memory, exam crunch, and company while you study.

Why study rooms · A trend, not a guess

“Learn with me” was already how students studied.

Our EdTech marketing agent flagged it: study-with-me streams were trending on TikTok and Instagram — students working alongside strangers for company and accountability. We built that feeling into the app.

3.5B#studywithme views on TikTok, 2022
700M+views on one Lofi Girl study stream, 2024
14.7Msubscribers to that single channel

Studying alongside peers lowers test anxiety and raises self-efficacy — Frontiers in Psychology, 2025.

Layer 03 · Reach

Break the sameness.

Every competitor looked the same, and our old mascots read young — cute, childish, tool-like. Nothing a high-schooler would keep on their phone.

A cast that feels like a school.

Three tutors with real personalities, grown out of the Answer.AI logo. Their voice carries into how each AI tutor talks, so the app feels like a place students belong.

Layer 04 · Personal

Streaks, nudges, and a school that knows you.

Step 1Collect data

Ace, Alma and Grant learn each student’s goals, strengths and progress. Every conversation and scanned problem adds to a shared student profile.

Step 2Act with the data

They use that context to act first — checking in after a tough question, previewing tomorrow’s class, or connecting students with the right support.

A light reward system keeps it going: scanning problems, mastering topics and finishing Ace’s exercises earn badges — plus random outfit drops for an avatar that shows up in the online study room.
