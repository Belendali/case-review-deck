The bet

Wall 01 · Worldbuilding

Ask the right five questions first.

PainPeople have a picture, not rules. One prompt in, something else comes out.
We builtOnboarding as five questions — move, goal, pressure, world, twist — with a live preview that reacts to every answer, and a Game Plan to confirm before anything is built.
AI's jobPlanner compiles the answers into an executable plan. The voice skill tells it how to ask: align → plan → confirm → build. Never runs ahead.

Wall 02 · Art

The game is the style guide.

PainCreators can't draw and can't name a style — and generated art loves to invent.
We builtArtist owns scene assets. At publish, a cover skill makes the cover from three inputs — scene, preview, intent — under one contract: transform, don't invent.
AI's jobTaste written as rules and a reference library: seven scene → cover pairs, every approved result goes back in.
