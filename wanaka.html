Wanaka

Anyone can build the game in their head — with AI as a teammate at every step.

User problem

Everyone has a game in their head.
Three walls keep it there.

Making a game takes three crafts most people never learned.

“I can’t code. How does a rule in my head become something I can actually play?”
“I don’t know how to draw or build 3D models at all.”
“I can see the game… but not the rules, the levels, how it all hangs together.”

The idea stays an idea.

The bet

Let AI carry the crafts, so people only bring the idea.

Describe the game. A crew of AI cats takes each wall: Developer and Tester make it play, Artist makes it look right, Planner shapes the world. The creator directs.

Wall 03 · Worldbuilding

Ask the right five questions first.

PainPeople have a picture, not rules. One prompt in, something else comes out.
We builtOnboarding as five questions — move, goal, pressure, world, twist — with a live preview that reacts to every answer, and a Game Plan to confirm before anything is built.
AI's jobPlanner compiles the answers into an executable plan. The voice skill tells it how to ask: align → plan → confirm → build. Never runs ahead.
