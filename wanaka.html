The AI workflow

How I work with AI, day to day.

Direction → feasibility → execution → QA → back into the system. AI is in every step; the judgment stays mine.

01Set direction

Explore directions and gather references with AI. A daily agent follows the market and competitors, so decisions start from evidence.

02Test feasibility

With the direction clear, AI + Figma on our design system produce quick demos — to see what is actually executable before committing.

03Execute

Key pages are designed by hand in Figma. AI drafts the edge cases and generates the motion, animation and asset variants around them.

04Ship & QA

Flows become working demos on GitHub; the team reviews the real thing. A Design QA skill lets engineers self-check tokens and spacing before review.

05Compound

New components that prove valuable go back into the design system. For anything generative, written skills keep output quality stable.
